# Prompt pack

Short prompts for ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot or any assistant that does not read skill folders. Paste one as your first message, then answer its questions. For the full procedure, paste the matching `SKILL.md` instead.

Every prompt keeps the same rules: you write first and the assistant challenges; "confirmed" means the right person at the customer agreed to a playback or recap, or acted; the assistant never moves a stage or a forecast category; anything not discussed stays empty.

## Before a meeting

```
Help me prepare a customer meeting. Before you suggest anything, ask me to write my own hypothesis in three lines: why change, why now, why us, in the customer's words. Then challenge it with the facts I give you, and only those facts. Show what we know per MEDDPICC letter, one score each from 0 to 3, set by its weakest part (0 not asked, 1 assumed, 2 relayed, 3 confirmed and tested), with the source for each fact or "unverified". Give me three open questions for the two weakest letters, one about the cost of doing nothing, and the next step to ask for with a date.
```

## After a call

```
Write a debrief of this customer call for my sign-off. First ask me which two MEDDPICC letters are weakest in my view, and write my answer down. Then, from the notes below, quote the customer's exact words for each letter with who said it, and give it one score from 0 to 3, set by its weakest part. A 3 needs the right person and a test; for the champion, an action they took for us. Write "Not discussed" where the notes say nothing. List what we promised and what they promised, and the next step with a date. Do not change the deal's stage. [paste notes]
```

## The recap mail

```
First ask me, in two or three lines, what I heard as the customer's main problem and what it costs. Then draft a "what we heard" mail to the customer from these notes, in their words, at most three bullets per section: Background, Challenges (with what they cost), Positive business outcomes, Minimum required capabilities, Reverse timeline (planned back from their compelling event). Open with the agreed next step. Mark anything the notes do not support as [check]. No product pitch. I will edit and send it myself. [paste notes]
```

## Deal review

```
Review this deal with me letter by letter on MEDDPICC. First ask which two letters I think are weakest, and why, and write my answer down. Then, for each letter, ask me for the customer moment behind it: a call moment, their mail, or their reply to our recap. No moment, no score above 1. Give each letter one score from 0 to 3 (0 not asked, 1 assumed, 2 relayed, 3 confirmed and tested; a champion gets 3 only for an action, and a promise is not one), set by the weakest part of its question. Do not add the scores up; find the two weakest letters and ask me the questions that test them. If pain is under 3 or the champion under 2, say it is not a deal yet. End with actions: what, who, when.
```

## Forecast category

```
Suggest a forecast category for this deal. First ask me which category I would call and why. Score pain, champion, economic buyer and paper process 0 to 3 (0 not asked, 1 assumed, 2 relayed, 3 confirmed and tested; a champion gets 3 only for an action, and a promise is not one). A visible opportunity needs pain at 3 and a potential champion at 2 or more. Commit (will close this quarter) needs the economic buyer, the paper process and a champion who has acted, all confirmed by the customer. Upside (may close this quarter) needs a champion who has acted. Future will not close this quarter. Black hole: qualified out or no move in 30 days. The stage sets the ceiling (01 none, 02 and 03 future, 04 and 05 upside, 06 commit, 07 won) and the letters can only lower it. Tell me what evidence would move it up. I decide.
```

## Champion or coach

```
Help me test whether my contact is a champion or a coach. First ask me what they have done for us since our last review and what I think their personal win is, and write my answer down. Then list what they did for us since our last review, each with its proof, and keep promises in a separate list: a promise to act is not an action. Check power, influence and their personal win. Then suggest one small ask with a date that only a champion could do, such as a meeting with the economic buyer. I decide what they are.
```

## QBR

```
Prepare a QBR for this account. First ask me how the account stands and which two parts are weakest, and write my answer down. Use only numbers the customer measured or confirmed. Open with their value pyramid and what changed, then last quarter (what we agreed, numbers beaten and missed), then next quarter: new goals the customer names, what missing each would cost, and how we measure them. Check the champion is still in role and acting. Keep support cases and feature requests out of the QBR. Flag if the renewal is within two quarters.
```

## Value map refresh

```
Refresh our value map from this quarter's customer calls and QBR notes. First ask me which drivers I think moved this quarter. Per driver, collect the customers' exact words with source and whether we may use them, and results only where the customer confirmed the number. List reasons customers gave that fit no driver, and drivers nobody mentioned. Propose at most three changes with their evidence. The leadership team decides.
```

## Practise with a buyer

```
Play the buyer in a [discovery call / new business meeting] so I can practise. Here is the brief: [the pain in numbers, what they need, objections]. Stay in role, never try to trip me up, reveal the pain only to good questions, and push back if I talk about the product too early. When I say stop, first ask which two points I think went weakest, then give feedback on: opens with their challenges; open, two-sided questions; needs in their words; numbers on the pain; proof that fits; plays back and gets agreement; questions that lead to our strengths; a next step with a date. Then two things you liked and two I could do differently.
```
