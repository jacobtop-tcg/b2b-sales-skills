description: Tests whether a contact in a B2B deal is a champion or a coach by what they did, not what they said or promised. Use before a proof of value, before a forecast call, or whenever the seller says "my champion".

# Champion check

A champion has power, influence and a personal win, and sells for you when you are not in the room. A coach is not a champion, and most deals have plenty of coaches.

## Rules you keep

- Only actions count: something they did for us outside our meetings, such as booking, introducing, or sharing the decision process in writing. Answers on a call are information. Plans, promises, friendliness and attention are not actions.
- Every action needs its proof: what they did, when, and how we know. Put quotation marks only around words the inputs quote; reported speech stays reported. A document you have not seen is named, not described.
- You list and test. The seller decides who is a champion: never write that someone is or is not one. Leave stages and forecast categories out; the seller and their leader decide them.
- "Not discussed" stays "Not discussed". A number the customer did not give stays empty; quote one they gave exactly, with its qualifier and period.
- Keep each fact at its strength: a relay stays "[name] said, [date]", a goal is not their personal win until they say so, and silent notes mean "no sign as of [date]", never "not done".
- No judgements about anyone's feelings, tone or confidence. Only what was said and done.
- Say each fact once, where it belongs.

## Steps

1. **Seller first.** Ask, without suggesting an answer: what has this contact done for us since the last review, and what is their personal win? Write down everything the seller says, in their words, before you test. Ask once more for a missing part, then write "not given"; never fill it from the notes. Ask the same about any other contact the notes show acting.
2. **Who.** Name, role, and what they own in the customer's words, or "not stated". A company fact is not their scope, and a replacement inherits nothing until someone says so. A contact who has left gets one line, no test, and an ask: introduce their successor and say who decides now.
3. **Actions since the last review.** If we owe them something they asked for, say so first. Then what, when, the proof, for this contact only. Promises go in a separate list.
4. **The test.** For a contact nobody has met, write "not met yet" and make the first meeting the next ask. Otherwise answer each with yes, no or not known, nothing else, then the evidence. Answer no only when the notes show it did not happen.
   - Do they have power, influence and a personal win? Power means the people who decide act on what they say, not that they sign.
   - Have we heard them explain why change, why now and why us to their colleagues? Name the moment.
   - Do they act between our meetings without being chased?
   - Have they taken us to the economic buyer?
   - Have they done something for us that cost them something?
   - Is our champion stronger than the competitor's?
5. **The personal win.** Fear or greed: what makes them act? Promotion, a new role to make a mark in, being measured on the project, something to lose if nothing changes, curiosity. Do not accept the first answer.
6. **A mistaken profile?** The nice one, the one who gives the most attention, someone with no power to move the deal, someone sponsoring several vendors. Name only signs backed by a dated thing they did or said. Consent to record, small talk, talk time and hedges like "I'll try" are not signs.
7. **The next small ask.** Something only a champion can do, with a date: a meeting with the economic buyer, bringing in another department, sharing the decision process. Next time, check that same action and date. If not, the reading would drop to coach so far; recorded actions stay. The seller decides.
8. **Three champions.** Note whether the deal also has a technical champion (owned by the SE) and a paper process champion who gets the contract through finance and legal and knows who approves the first invoice.

## Output

```
CONTACT: [name], [role]
ACTIONS (with proof): [...]
PROMISES (not actions): [...]
TEST: [six lines, yes / no / not known, with evidence]
PERSONAL WIN: [...]
WARNING SIGNS: [...]
NEXT ASK: [what, by when]
READING: acted for us / potential champion / coach so far / not met yet / left the company. You decide.
OTHER CHAMPIONS: technical [...], paper process [...]
```

Acted for us: an action with proof. Potential champion: the role, a stated reason to act and a promise still inside its date. Coach so far: information only, or a promise past its date.
