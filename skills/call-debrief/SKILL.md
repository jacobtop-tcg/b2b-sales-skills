# Call debrief

Reflect on the meeting while it is fresh, as a team, and keep learning how this customer decides.

## Rules you keep

- The people who were in the meeting reflect first. Ask them, then read the notes. Your draft adds to their view.
- Quote the customer's own words for what we learned, with who said them. Keep our notes and our reading of them apart from what the customer said.
- Use MEDDICC as a lens on their decision. Say which part a point sheds light on (pain, metrics, economic buyer, decision criteria, decision process, paper process, champion, competition). No scores and no ranking of letters.
- Be sceptical about champions. A friendly, helpful contact is a coach until they act for us. Write what each person did, and who only gave advice.
- Never fill a gap with a guess. Anything not discussed belongs under what we still need to get in place.
- Three is a guide. If there are only two honest points, write two.
- No judgements about anyone's feelings or tone. Only what was said and done.
- If the input is a recording or transcript, ask whether everyone was told and agreed. If nobody knows, flag it at the top.

## Steps

1. Ask the team what went well and what they would do differently. Write their answers down before you read anything.
2. Read the notes or the transcript.
3. **What we did well:** up to three things in how we ran the meeting (the questions we asked, how we listened, what we showed and when, the next step we asked for), each with the moment it shows in.
4. **What we can do better:** up to three, each written as something to do next time.
5. **What we learned about the customer:** three things we now understand about how they decide, in their words, with who said them and the part of MEDDICC each sheds light on.
6. **What we still need to get in place:** three things we do not know yet or are guessing about, each with who at the customer could tell us and how we find out in the next conversation.
7. **Next step:** what was agreed with the customer, who and when. If nothing was agreed, say so.

## Output

```
RECORDING: consent confirmed / not known / no recording

```
