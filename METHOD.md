# The method on one page

Every skill in this pack runs on the same few rules. They are here once, so you can read them before you install anything.

## One idea

Everyone in the company sees each deal the way the buyer does, and trusts only what the customer has confirmed. The question that tests it is: how do you know?

## The evidence ladder

Score what you know, not how you feel about it.

| Score | Word | Means |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Not asked | We do not know. |
| 1 | Assumed | Our own belief. Nothing from the customer. |
| 2 | Relayed | Heard from one person. Not tested. |
| 3 | Confirmed | Confirmed by the right person and tested: they agreed to a playback or recap, or acted. Agreeing to prepare a playback is not a test. |

For the champion, a 3 means they acted for us. A promise to act, or an answer on a call, is not an action.

Give each letter one score: the score of the weakest part of its question in the table below. A yes with a condition still open is not a 3. Look at the two weakest letters, never a total. A deal with a high total and a weak champion is still a weak deal.

## The eight letters (MEDDPICC)

| Letter | What it is | The question |
|---|---|---|
| Identify pain | The business need that makes them act. | Is it big, measurable and urgent? Who feels it? |
| Metrics | How success is measured, in numbers. | Which numbers, today and target? |
| Champion | Sells for you inside the customer. | What have they done for us since the last review? |
| Economic buyer | Owns the budget and the final yes. | Have we met them? Do they agree with the case? |
| Decision criteria | What they will judge us on, in their words. | Which of our differences are among them? |
| Decision process | The steps, people and timing to a decision. | Who is involved at each step, and when? |
| Paper process | How the contract gets signed. | Who signs, which reviews, how long, and any term outside our standard? |
| Competition | Who or what else wants the budget, including doing nothing. | Why would they choose us, and why not? |

## The 3 whys

Why change? Why now? Why us? In the customer's words. If the seller cannot tell them that way, the deal is back in discovery.

## Stages and their exit

A deal moves when the customer has done something, never when we have.

| Stage | It moves on when |
|---|---|
| 01 Leads and first meeting | The customer confirms the pain. |
| 02 Discovery and analysis | After the new business meeting, the champion has done something for us: booked the economic buyer, or brought in people of power. |
| 03 Solution design | The champion names us the vendor of choice and can defend the 3 whys. |
| 04 Executive alignment | The economic buyer sponsors the next step, and the playback is booked. |
| 05 Validation | The economic buyer and the champion confirm the business case. |
| 06 Proposal and negotiation | Executive sign-off and signature. |
| 07 Implement, launch and adopt | The customer confirms the first result from the case. |

## Forecast categories

A deal enters the forecast as a **visible opportunity**: pain at 3 and a potential champion at 2 or more. Under that, it is not a deal yet.

| Category | Means | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Commit | Will close this quarter. | The economic buyer, the paper process and a champion who has acted, all confirmed by the customer. |
| Upside | May close this quarter. | A champion who has acted. |
| Future | Will not close this quarter. | A visible opportunity. |
| Black hole | Qualified out, or no move in 30 days. | Nothing. Qualifying a deal out is a win. |

The stage sets the highest category a deal can have: stages 01 none, 02 and 03 future, 04 and 05 upside, 06 commit, 07 won. The letters can only lower it.

## The rules for AI

1. AI drafts. People decide.
2. The seller writes first, then AI challenges. Never the other way round.
3. "Confirmed" belongs to the customer. The seller signs off a draft; that does not confirm anything.
4. Every 2 or 3 has a customer moment behind it: a call moment, their mail, or their reply to "what we heard". No moment, no score above 1.
5. AI never moves a stage or a forecast category. It says what evidence would.
6. "Not discussed" stays empty. A fact without a source is marked unverified.
7. No scores of feelings, tone or confidence on people.
8. Every message that goes out under a person's name is read and sent by that person.
9. Ask before you record, offer to stop, and keep recordings only as long as you need them.
